Who Are We?

Kindernest Preschool was founded to create a loving, safe, and stimulating environment where children take their first steps into learning.

Our Mission

We believe in play-based learning to nurture confident, curious, and kind children.

Our Values

Every child deserves to feel safe, valued, and loved at all times.

We nurture mind, body, and heart to help children grow confidently and creatively.

Parents are partners in building a strong, supportive foundation for every learner.

"Children are not things to be molded, but are people to be unfolded." — Jess Lair
